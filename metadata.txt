about=Specialized tools from the UTN Forest Observatory for active wildfire monitoring (NASA FIRMS) and high-resolution base maps.
    .
    Provides an agile and fully native geospatial ecosystem for forest and wildfire management:
    .
    🔥 Active Wildfires: 1-click loading of near real-time thermal hotspots (NASA FIRMS MODIS and VIIRS) with official NASA temporal symbology, auto-reprojection to EPSG:4326, automatic Ecuador framing, and dynamic 5-minute background auto-refresh.
    .
    🗺️ Base Maps: Instant access to high-resolution satellite imagery and cartographic base layers (Google Hybrid, Google Road, Bing Satellite, OSM Standard, and OpenTopoMap) automatically placed at the bottom of the layer stack.
    .
    🌐 Visit our official web platform: https://observatorioforestal.utn.edu.ec/
